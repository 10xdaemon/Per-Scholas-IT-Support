# Supporting Mobile Devices

## 9.1 MOBILE DEVICES AND PERIPHERALS

### Mobile Devices Accessories

- Digitizer `touchscreen` and gesture support: Trackpad/touchpad
- Stylus
- Microphone
- Camera/webcam
- Speakers
- Headset

### Mobile Device Wired Connection Methods

- Ports
    - USB-C, micro, mini
    - Serial
    - Video: {HDMI, VGA, Thunderbolt}

### Port Replicators and Docking Stations

- Port replicator
- Docking station **More stationary**
    - Laptop connects to station and can be interacted with as if were a desktop system
    - May add additional connections or support for expansion cards


### Wi-Fi Netowrking

- Enabling/disabling Wi-Fi
    - Switch or function key combination
    - Settings menu
- Airplane mode
- Wi-Fi connector placement: Edges of display

### Cellular Data Networking

- 3G- 7.2 Mbps
- 4G `LTE- 300 Mbps`
- 5G- 10 Gbps
- Subscriber Identity Module (SIM): Identifies device on mobile network
- eSIM: Electronic version of SIM card allowing multiple profiles for single device

### Mobile Hotspots and Thetering

- Tethering
    - USB connection
- Hotspot **When you turn your phone into a hotspot, it is considered an `access point`**
    - Peer-to-peer Wi-Fi connection

### Bluethoth Wireless Connections

- 2.4 GHz frequency band
- Wireless connectivity
- Pairing process: Confirmation of connection to a device

### Near-Field Communication Wireless Connections

- NFC connectivity
- Contactless payment system `tokenization`
- Device configuration and launching of applications

## 9.2 MOBILE APPS AND DATA

### Mobile Apps

- iOS apps- downloadable from Apple's app store: Xcode IDE and Swift language
- Android apps- downloadable from Google Play store and third-party sites
- App permissions- allow access to location, networks, camera, microphone, etc. 

### Account Setup

- Apple ID
- Google
- Synchronize settings and data between multiple devices

### Types of Data to Synchronize

1. Contacts
1. Calendar
1. Mail
1. Multimedia
1. Documents
1. Apps
1. Passwords

### Email Configuration Options

- Commercial email: Google, Outlook, Office, Yahoo, etc.
- Enterprise email
    1. Microsoft Exchange
    1. ActiveSync
- AutoDiscover
- Manual
    1. SMTP
    1. IMAP
    1. POP3

### Synchronization Methods

- Sync to PC: USB or wireless (Pops up as a storage device)
- Sync to Automobile: CarPlay, Android Auto

### Enterprise Mobility Management

- EMM- Enterprise Mobility Management
    - `MDM`- Mobile Device Management: Networks, hardware use, reset and remote wipe
    - `MAM`- Mobile Application Management: Application settings and control `don't have a company phone` AKA `BYOD - bring you own device`
- Apple Business Manager
- Managed Google Play

### Location Services

- Global positioning system
    - Satellite connectivity to determine location
- Indoor positioning system `three towers`- Triangulating
    - Cellular towers, Wi-Fi access points, and Bluetooth/RFID beacons
